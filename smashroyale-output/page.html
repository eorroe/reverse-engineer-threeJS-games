Controls

WASD or arrows to move. Mouse to look. Left click fires; right click aims. The marksman rifle uses a 4× scope. Space drops from the carrier or jumps. Shift sprints. Tap C or Ctrl to crouch or stand; hold to go prone. Press while sprinting to slide, then release to keep moving. Tap again from prone to crouch; jump or sprint to stand. Z also toggles prone. Hold E or right click to aim. F opens doors and chests or collects gear. R reloads. H uses a med kit; J uses a shield. Press the same supply key again to cancel. Q cycles weapons; 1–3 selects a weapon. Tab opens the scoreboard. Escape pauses.

Controller: left stick moves, right stick looks, triggers aim and fire, bottom face button jumps. Connect a controller and press a button for its full control labels.
